Story Stack Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 17, 2026

Story Stack is a self-hosted audiobook library application. This policy explains what information Story Stack stores and how it is used when you use a Story Stack server or the Story Stack mobile app.

Who controls your information

Story Stack can be hosted by an individual or organization. The operator of the Story Stack server you connect to controls that server, its user accounts, and its data retention. For questions about a particular server, contact that server operator. For questions about this application, contact privacy@storystacknott.com.

Information stored by Story Stack

  • Account information: your name or display name, email address or login identifier, role, password hash, and account status.
  • Library activity: books added to your library, shelves, ratings, reviews, bookmarks, finished status, and playback position.
  • Connection information: the server address and connection settings needed to connect your app to a Story Stack server.
  • Technical data: limited request and error information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the server. The server operator may also receive standard network information from requests.

How information is used

Story Stack uses this information to authenticate users, provide library and store features, save playback across devices, manage shelves and reviews, deliver audiobook files, synchronize offline activity, and maintain the service. Story Stack does not sell personal information or display advertising.

Audio files and offline listening

A Story Stack server reads audiobook files from the folders selected by its operator and serves authorized files to signed-in users. When you download a book for offline listening, the audio file and related book information are stored on your device until you remove them. Playback progress and offline changes may remain on the device and sync to the server when a connection is available.

Metadata and third-party services

When a user requests a metadata search, Story Stack may send book search terms to external catalog providers, such as Audible or Google Books, to find title, author, description, genre, duration, and cover information. Story Stack does not send your password or private library contents to these providers. Their use of information is governed by their own privacy policies.

Remote access may use Cloudflare Tunnel or another reverse proxy selected by the server operator. Cloudflare or that proxy may process network traffic according to its own privacy policy.

Sharing

Your account and library activity may be visible to the operator of the Story Stack server and, where the server permits it, to other users of that server. Story Stack does not provide a central hosted social network or sell user data to third parties.

Security and retention

Passwords are stored as hashes rather than plain text, and authenticated access is required for server features. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. The server operator decides how long server data is retained and is responsible for backups, hosting security, and access management.

Your choices

You can remove downloaded books from your device, change your password where enabled, remove books and reviews from your library, and ask the server operator to correct or delete your account data. Requests involving a self-hosted server must be made to that server's operator.

Children's privacy

Story Stack is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Story Stack's features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
